# Study Cards — Privacy Policy

_Effective date: September 27, 2026_

This policy explains what information the Study Cards mobile app ("the app", "we") handles and why.

## Information stored on your device

The words, meanings and starred cards you add are saved only on your device. We do not have servers, we do not collect this content, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Advertising

The app shows ads served by **Google AdMob**, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

- the device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA),
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language),
- interactions with ads (views and taps),
- diagnostic and crash information related to ad delivery.

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

- [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

We do not receive or store this information ourselves.

## Your choices and consent

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** before ads are personalized, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent form. You can refuse, and you can change your choice later.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: _Settings → Google → Ads_; iOS: _Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking_).
- If you do not consent to personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

Your study content never leaves your device through the app. Data processed by Google AdMob is handled under Google's security practices.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
